What we do

Three programmes that work together, so no one falls through the gap between school and home.

Homework clubFour afternoons a week with volunteer tutors, in libraries across the city.
Neighbourhood teamsFree basketball and football teams. Training only counts if homework is done.
One-to-one mentorsA trained volunteer who meets each teenager every week, for at least two years.
